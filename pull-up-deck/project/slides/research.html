05 · Validating the problem

We ask about last Tuesday, not about our app

Interview guide · 10 to 15 students

  1. Tell me about the last time you wanted to go out but couldn’t find anyone.
  2. What did you end up doing instead?
  3. Have you skipped something because you didn’t want to go alone?
  4. What would make you comfortable joining people you don’t know?
  5. What have you already tried: groups, apps, clubs?

We never ask “would you use PULL UP.?” People say yes to hypothetical products.
