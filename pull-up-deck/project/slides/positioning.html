07 · Positioning

For students who want to go out but can’t find free company, PULL UP. is a same-day plan board that turns free time into real meetups, unlike group chats or friend apps, because every post is a plan with a time, a place and open spots.

Activity first

You join momos, not a stranger’s profile. Friendship is the by-product.

Now, not someday

Plans expire when their time passes. No feed to scroll, nothing to stockpile.

No weird energy

Verified campus email, host approval, leave any time, report and block.

Read the positioning statement out loud. Then the three product principles, which are how we decide what to build and what to refuse. Activity first: we never ask anyone to present themselves as lonely. Now, not someday: posts expire, so the board always shows what is actually possible tonight. No weird energy: safety is a release requirement, not a feature.
