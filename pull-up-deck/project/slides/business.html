Finding company stays free. Venues pay when we bring the crowd

First

Venue partnerships. Cafés and courts pay for clearly labelled offers, once repeat use is shown.

Then

Booking commission. A fee when a squad books a paid court or table through a partner.

Later

Promoted plans and club tools. Only once the board is busy enough that visibility matters.
