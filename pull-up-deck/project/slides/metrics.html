Our north star is plans that happened per week, confirmed by at least two members in the check-in. It breaks into three levers we can work on separately: supply of plans, fill rate and show-up rate. If the north star drops, the driver tree tells us which lever broke. Guardrails stop us from growing in an unsafe way, and the anti-metric keeps us honest: time in app going up means we are becoming a feed, which is the opposite of the mission.
