Four signature features, each mapped to a drop-off in the funnel. Drop a vibe fixes the awkward ask, because you post an activity instead of inviting a person. The Now Board fixes timing. Squad Lock fixes “maybe next week” by turning interest into a confirmed group. The check-in closes the loop and gives us our north star data. The Regulars and Vibe Match wait until we have proven the core loop.
