07

Two axes that students actually decide on: is this planned ahead or happening now, and is it with people I already know or new people. Meetup and Timeleft are planned ahead. WhatsApp and Instagram are people you know. Playo is the honest closest competitor: it already lets you find players for a game. Our differentiation is narrower and testable: any activity, same day, inside one verified campus. These are positioning hypotheses, not a claim that nobody does this.
