Riskiest assumption

Enough students will join a same-day plan posted by someone they barely know, and actually turn up.

Step 1 · Concierge. WhatsApp community plus a form; we match plans by hand.

Step 2 · Closed pilot. 20 to 30 students on the app, one week.

Step 3 · Campus beta. Open to the campus only when gates pass.

12

If one thing kills PULL UP., it is this assumption: that people will join a near-stranger’s plan the same day and show up. So we test it in the cheapest way first, a concierge pilot run by hand over WhatsApp, before writing much code. The four gates decide whether we move to the next step. Zero unresolved safety reports is non-negotiable.
