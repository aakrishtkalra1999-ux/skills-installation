PULL UP. · PM Final Project · 31 Oct 2026 · Pilot schedule is a plan, not a traction claim

13

A social app has the classic cold-start problem: nobody joins if no plans exist, and nobody posts if nobody is there. Our answer is to shrink the map until it feels full: one campus, three activity types, two time windows. A board with eight plans at 7 pm feels alive; a city with eight plans feels dead. Partner with student clubs and sports teams to post real plans. Never fabricate activity. Only report traction you actually measured.
