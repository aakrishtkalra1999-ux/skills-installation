05

The biggest mistake would be launching for everyone who is ever bored. A social app needs enough people free at the same time in the same place. One campus gives us density, shared locations, verifiable email and a safety environment we can actually moderate. Sports players scored second, but Playo already serves them well. The scores are our judgement and will be revisited after interviews.
