# Deck design: Business Tangent 2026 (KAP FPO case)

Written with Superpowers brainstorming. Approve this before the deck is built.

## Intent
A 6-slide answer (plus title, thank-you and appendix slides) that a St. Stephen's FnIC judge can score against the four deliverables in under 5 minutes. Every number comes from `model/KAP_FPO_Model.xlsx` through `deck/model_data.json`, so slides and model can't drift apart.

## Goals
- One slide per deliverable, with slide 1 giving the answer and the scorecard.
- Maize vs vegetable differences visible on every main slide.
- Calculations shown in the appendix, as the rules ask.

## Non-goals
- No new analysis. Content is Part D of `final/Working_Draft_and_Final.md` with option A.
- No per-FPO assessment (the case says it isn't needed).

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it looks good)
- Any number on a slide that differs from the model.
- More than 6 main slides, or missing team details on the PDF (disqualifying).
- Overclaiming: "all 12 self-sufficient" or "all reach ₹1 crore".

## Constraints
- Deadline 28 Oct, 6:00 PM. One submission only.
- Cover must show team name, participant names, email IDs, college names (filled in from your details; placeholders until then).
- Plain Indian English, zero em or en dashes, every abbreviation defined once.

## Approaches considered
1. **Kit consulting style (Armageddon look): chosen as my call.** Navy and white, full-sentence headlines, a source line on every slide, built-in charts. Proven, fast, and judges in finance societies read it easily.
2. A themed design from a design-system shortlist. Nicer looking, but it costs a review round we don't have time for before the deadline.
3. PowerPoint (.pptx). Editable by hand, but numbers would no longer flow from the model.
