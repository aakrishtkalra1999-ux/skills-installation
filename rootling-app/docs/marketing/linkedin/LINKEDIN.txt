🌱 Introducing Rootling: little roots, big words.

Millions of Indian families live abroad. Their children often understand the family language but reply in English, and video calls with grandparents shrink to "hi" and "bye".

Rootling is a web app that helps children aged 3–10 speak Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Gujarati or Malayalam:
→ 5-minute lessons with pictures and sounds (no reading needed for under-6s)
→ "Tap and say it": every lesson ends with the child saying a real line to their grandparent
→ Personalised with the child's name and their own word for grandma (Paati, Ammamma, Dadi, Nani…)
→ A parents area with the words to practise at home
→ No ads, no trackers, parent-owned accounts

Built as my final project for AI in Business - II at Masters' Union, with React, Supabase, Stripe and Vercel. It's live and free to start:
👉 https://www.rootling.space

If you're a parent abroad, or know one, I'd love your feedback. Which language should we add next?
