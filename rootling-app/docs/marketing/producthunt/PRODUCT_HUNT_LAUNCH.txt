Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I built Rootling because so many Indian families abroad have the same story: the kids understand their grandparents perfectly, but they answer in English. Calls home shrink to "hi" and "bye", and most language apps are built for adults, for Hindi or Spanish, and for people who can already read.

Rootling is for children aged 3–10:
🌱 5-minute lessons with pictures and sounds (no reading needed for under-6s)
💬 "Tap and say it": every lesson ends with the child saying a real line, like "வணக்கம், பாட்டி!" (Hello, Paati!)
👵 Personalised with the child's name and their own word for grandma: Paati, Ammamma, Dadi, Nani, Baa…
📖 Bedtime stories that star the child and their grandparent
👪 A parents area showing the words to practise at home

Tamil is live with a full course; Telugu, Punjabi, Gujarati and Malayalam are in preview while native speakers review every line. It's free to start, and there are no ads or trackers anywhere children play.

I'd love your feedback, especially from parents: which language should we build next? 🙏
